## Not written yet

Three guides wait on a worked example, because neither sample does the job yet and a guide here
only shows code that runs:

- **Collision.** The engine has layers and masks, a broad phase, a narrow phase that reports
  contact normals and depths, and resolution, all tested. Until a sample uses them, the headers in
  <RepoLink path="engine/collision">engine/collision/</RepoLink> and
  [the tick](/docs/concepts/tick/#a-scenes-four-phases) are the guide.
- **Audio.** Sound banks load from the manifest and play through `SoundBank`, but there is no
  sound in the repository to play.
- **Local multiplayer.** Split-screen is covered in
  [Drawing, views and cameras](/docs/concepts/drawing/#split-screen); a sample with more than one
  player is planned.
