# The flash check's match (samples/linesweeper/README.md, The bursts against
# WCAG 2.3.1): website.txt's placements with its captures and the waits before
# them taken out, run with --sequence, so every presentation update of four
# tetrises, one single and the top-out is written as a 60 fps frame.
#
# It is website.txt as fast as a script can play it - every press is one tick
# down and one tick up, and nothing waits between pieces - because flashes per
# second are what is being measured, and a faster player puts the bursts closer
# together. footage.txt is the same match at a viewer's pace.
