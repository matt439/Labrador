# What the five backends actually do, axis by axis

> Read-only sweep by 350 agents, 2026-08-28, against the tree at `ca5b2e3`.
> Part of the [five-backend equivalence sweep](README.md). Nothing was built and
> nothing was run: every claim comes from reading source. **This document is the
> sweep as written and is not updated as findings are fixed** — see the README on
> why that disclaimer is load-bearing and what carries the live state instead.

This is the reference half of the sweep and the part with the longest useful
life. It is the 2026-08-19 audit's table re-run: every one of its 29 axes
re-checked against the tree as it stands, and extended to the two backends that
did not exist when it was written — plus eleven axes it could not have had,
because they are terms only D3D12 and Vulkan introduce.

Every row is either a real difference between the five backends that **no caller
can observe**, or one the seam explicitly delegates to a backend. Rows whose
verdict is not one of those two are written up in the [README](README.md).
