# Documentation drift in `engine/render/`, at five backends

> Read-only sweep by 350 agents, 2026-08-28, against the tree at `ca5b2e3`.
> Part of the [five-backend equivalence sweep](README.md). Nothing was built and
> nothing was run: every claim comes from reading source. **This document is the
> sweep as written and is not updated as findings are fixed** — see the README on
> why that disclaimer is load-bearing and what carries the live state instead.

The design documents are authoritative on intent and change by amendment in the
same commit as the change that fights them ([CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md),
[PHILOSOPHY.md](../../design/PHILOSOPHY.md)). The comments in `engine/render/`
are held to the same standard — `renderer.h` is not a header with comments, it is
the specification of the seam, and several of its paragraphs are the only place a
rule is written down.

**This is a work item, not a fix.** Nothing in this sweep changed a comment,
because a review that quietly rewrites the thing it is reviewing cannot be
checked afterwards. That rule is inherited from the 2026-08-19 audit and it is
the reason this document can be audited at all.

**113 claims the code no longer supports**, across twelve partitions covering all
91 files in `engine/render/` plus the four test files and the three CMake scripts
that describe them. By severity: 26 high, 60 medium, 27 low. By kind:
48 stale-backend-arithmetic, 21 behaviour, 20 count, 16 cross-reference, 5 dead-path, 3 other.

**The distribution is the finding.** `stale-backend-arithmetic` — a sentence that
counts the backends while legislating a term — is 48 of the 113, more than
the next two kinds together. These comments were written at two backends, amended
at three, and there are five.

Forty-seven were put through an adversarial check that defaulted to refuting them;
44 survived. The 94% survival rate is not a sign of a lax check — it is what
separates this document from [INVENTORY.md](INVENTORY.md). A drifted comment is
decidable by reading two files. A behavioural divergence needs a caller who can
tell, and 61 of the 65 candidates raised on that side died.

Findings marked *(unchecked)* were raised but not put through the adversarial
pass, which ran out of budget at 47. They are unexamined, not cleared.
