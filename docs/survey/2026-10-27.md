# Features other 2D engines have: the comparison of 2026-10-27

**The comparison as written, not updated as items land.** Each open item is a
`GAP#` row in [TODO.md](../../TODO.md), which is where its state lives; a row
the owner takes up becomes a story in
[BACKLOG.md](../product/BACKLOG.md) first, by that file's rule.

Labrador was compared with seven engines and frameworks a 2D C++ developer
would weigh against it: raylib, SDL3 with its satellite libraries,
MonoGame/FNA, LÖVE, Godot 4 (2D only), Defold and Bevy. Labrador's side was read
from the public headers, the website guides, PHILOSOPHY and BACKLOG at
`26e6597`. The other engines' side was written from knowledge of their current
releases and not re-checked against their documentation, so the counts below
are approximate. "Of 7" counts the engines that ship a feature in the core or
an official first-party library.

## What was left out

Everything PHILOSOPHY's Non-goals rule out: online play, 3D, an editor, a
scripting layer, and hot reload or reflection (T12). Everything a design rule
declines: rigid-body physics (T3), an engine action map (T1), autolayout UI
(UI), an ECS or node tree (Non-goals), a per-object sort (order is the
depth), mipmapped minification (SEAM.md §6), screen-reader narration (UI), and
platforms beyond Windows and the browser (android.md). And everything already
in BACKLOG: vibration `PI-21`, localisation `OCA-41`/`OCA-42`/`OCA-43`/`OCA-77`,
pointer menus `OCA-50`, a save location `OCA-76`, a join screen `OCA-46`, DPI
`OCA-66`, the cost of music `OCA-40`, the recording renderer `OCA-32`, replay
`REV#9` and a frame overlay `REV#10`.

## For the owner's decision

In the order of their value to a couch-multiplayer game. Sizes are BACKLOG's.

| Row | Gap | Of 7 | Size | What deciding it involves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `GAP#1` | PlayStation, Switch and generic pads on Windows; more than four | 7 | L | XInput reads only Xbox-style pads, and guests bring their own. Windows.Gaming.Input or GameInput (built), or SDL's gamepad layer (bought at the platform edge, T9). |
| `GAP#2` | Streamed, compressed music (Ogg or MP3) | 7 | L | XWB banks hold PCM or MS-ADPCM in memory, which bloats a browser download. A decoder and a streaming voice on XAudio2, Web Audio and null. After `OCA-40`. |
| `GAP#3` | Render to texture | 7 | L | Integer-scaled pixel art, transitions, minimaps. A target type, five backends and new goldens. |
| `GAP#4` | PNG and other common image formats | 7 | M | Removes texconv from the first hour. T9 allows buying the decoder; premultiply at load; a vcpkg change updates ReleaseNotices. |
| `GAP#5` | A clip rectangle inside a view | 7 | M | Scrolling lists and panels. One more state on the draw list, on five backends. |
| `GAP#6` | Circles, polygons and debug drawing | 5 | M | Rectangles and lines are `engine:white` already; non-quad geometry is not. Debug-drawing collision shapes is the usual need. |
| `GAP#7` | Textures made or updated from pixels at run time | 7 | M | `resource_factory.h` already makes a texture from decoded bytes; the question is exposing it to a game. |
| `GAP#8` | Easing functions | 4 | S | Headless and small. T1 asks for an extraction: check what LineSweeper and ColourWars already hand-roll. |
| `GAP#9` | Camera rotation | 5 | M | `Camera` is translation and scale. Culling's visible rectangle has to grow to cover a rotation. |
| `GAP#10` | A particle emitter as a component | 3 | S | Promote LineSweeper's field to `samples/components` only if a second client would copy it. |
| `GAP#11` | Tilemaps and Tiled import | 2 | M | Genre-specific: waits for a client that uses tiles (Non-goals, Every genre). |
| `GAP#12` | TrueType and OpenType fonts | 7 | L | MakeSpriteFont bakes `.spritefont` on Windows only. Better folded into `OCA-43` than a story of its own. |
| `GAP#13` | Relative mouse motion (raw input, pointer lock) | 7 | M | Left out of the cursor change: Win32 raw input, and a pointer lock the browser grants only inside an input event, as it does full screen. |

## Not now

Custom shaders and post-processing (XL, against T8's one pipeline), multiply
and screen blends, 2D lights and shadows, a screenshot to a file, skeletal
animation, positional audio and effect buses, clipboard, file drop and IME
composition, and grid pathfinding. Each waits for a client that needs it (T1).
