# Journeys

What each persona does with Labrador from first contact to the end of their goal, step by step, with what actually happens today. Taken on 2026-10-13 against `master` at `0977112`. Every journey was mapped by one agent and then walked by a second that opened every touchpoint and tried to refute the first; the steps below are the walked version. The gaps each step exposes are in [GAP-REGISTER.md](GAP-REGISTER.md).

A step's status is one of: works; **friction** (completes with knowledge nothing gives you); **broken**; **missing**; or *unverified*, where only hardware, a browser or a person can settle it.

### Evaluate Labrador and decide whether it fits my game

`oca-evaluate-and-decide` · 8 friction, 1 broken, 1 works, 1 missing

**Goal.** Learn quickly and honestly whether Labrador suits a local, possibly split-screen, 2D C++ game, and what each choice will cost, before spending any time on setup.

**Trigger.** They are choosing an engine or library for a 2D C++ game. They find the GitHub README or labradorengine.com, perhaps while comparing it with raylib, SDL, SFML or Godot.

**Success looks like.** Within one reading session they have a yes or no that every public page agrees with. A yes rests on evidence they can check: a sample that does what they need, the stated platform reach and the stated stability. A no comes with a clear reason and no time lost on a build.
